# Lecture plan: Epically Efficient EHR Tools (TLM 2026)

Show and tell, no live demo. One made-up patient, ZZTEST, LIVER (58M with cirrhosis, 14 years of records, 312 outside documents), carries the talk: a new referral and 10 minutes before you walk in.

## Decisions (Mohammad, October 2026)

- No QR code on the cover. No watercolor section pages: a small grey part label sits top left instead.
- No "what do you think they found?" prompts. The audience is specialists.
- Show our three real Synopsis views as mock-ups and say "we built 3, you can too". The liver disease view (his name for it, not "cirrhosis view") has no etiology workup; the chronic liver disease workup view has the MASLD section (weight, BMI, A1c, glucose, lipids); the post-transplant view follows our real view's order.
- Mock-ups stay readable: about 18 rows per screen. Long views scroll over several slides under one slide number, with the date header frozen.
- Charts use the muted palette in MY_STYLE.md.
- Nothing to disclose. No Tufts line on the AI slide.
