# Epically Efficient EHR Tools: background image prompts

## Visual concept

A hepatology clinic seen through its desk: the workstation where the chart lives, the bedside where the history used to live, and the pile of outside records a new referral brings. Quiet objects and one doctor seen from behind, no screens with readable content. Same watercolor look as the ALF deck.

## Palette

Warm ivory paper (#F4EEE2), muted slate (#5E6B78), ochre (#B98A4A), dusty rose (#B97D7D), sage (#8FA08A) lightly, cream (#EFE6D2). A small localized dark indigo (#2E3550) wash only behind the cover subject.

## Master style block

Clinical editorial watercolor over fine gray ink linework, on warm ivory textured cold-press paper. Loose, controlled washes with visible paper grain and soft blooms. Thin confident ink lines. Muted slate, ochre, dusty rose, sage and cream. One focal subject, large calm negative space. 16:9, full-bleed.

## Master negative prompt

No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks or signatures. No readable screen content, no user interface, no digits on monitors. No neon, glow, lens flare, 3D render look, glossy shading, photorealism, stock-photo look or infographic icons. No borders, frames, torn edges, visible paper edge, square crop, white margins or centered composition (for full-bleed images).

- cover: two monitors with blank, softly lit screens, keyboard, mouse, coffee cup. Screens must have no digits, windows or logos.
- historian: the same doctor (short dark hair, white coat, seen from behind or in profile, no detailed face). Left half bedside with a seated patient seen from behind; right half the doctor at a workstation. Patient depicted respectfully.
- records stack: a tall, slightly messy stack of paper charts and outside records with a few colored tabs and a binder clip. No writing on any page.
