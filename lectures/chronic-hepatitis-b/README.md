# Chronic Hepatitis B

Residents, GI fellows, hepatologists and NPs. One build.js makes both versions.

- `Chronic Hepatitis B (30 min).pptx` and `CHB-slides-30min.pdf`: core talk, 30 numbered slides plus a renal-dosing appendix
- `Chronic Hepatitis B.pptx` and `CHB-slides-60min.pdf`: extended talk, 56 numbered slides plus a renal-dosing appendix (full history, HDV, transplant, children, stopping evidence, pipeline)
- `LECTURE_PLAN.md`: outline, sources and open questions
- `build/`: build.js, figures, `sh rebuild.sh` builds both decks. Slides inside `ext(() => {...})` appear only in the 60-minute version.

Not in the repo: the reviewer dossier and Mohammad's own uploads. Cover, closing and two spot images were made with Gemini from chronic-hepatitis-b-background-image-prompts.md.
The HBsAg-based HCC model on the calculator is a 2026 research model and is labeled that way on the slide.
