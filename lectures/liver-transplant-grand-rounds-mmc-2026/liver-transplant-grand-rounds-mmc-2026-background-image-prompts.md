# Liver transplant grand rounds (Maine Medical Center): background image prompts

## Visual concept

The trip a patient from Maine makes to a transplant: the coast road south, the clinic where someone writes the referral, the waiting, the donor liver in its cooler, and the pill box afterwards. Ordinary objects, drawn the way a medical illustrator would, with no people in them. The cover and section pages are full-bleed ivory paper with the subject on one side; the one picture beside slide text (slide 19) is a spot illustration that fades into white.

## Palette

Same as the ALF/ACLF deck, so the two talks look like they came from the same hand:

- Warm ivory paper (#F4EEE2) as the base
- Muted slate (#5E6B78) for shadow and ink wash
- Ochre (#B98A4A) and dusty rose (#B97D7D) for the main subject
- Sage (#8FA08A) for pine, water and secondary structures, lightly
- Cream (#EFE6D2) for highlights
- No dark indigo in this deck: it is a referral talk, not an ICU one

## Master style block

Clinical editorial watercolor over fine gray ink linework, on warm ivory textured cold-press paper. Loose, controlled washes with visible paper grain and soft blooms at the edges. Ink lines are thin and confident, like a hand-drawn plate in an older atlas. Muted slate, ochre, dusty rose, sage and cream. One focal subject. Large calm negative space. Full-bleed composition with no border, frame, vignette edge or seam. Wide 16:9, at least 1920×1080.

## Master negative prompt

No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, road-sign lettering, logos, watermarks or signatures. No people, faces or hands. No blood, no gore, no surgical scene. No neon, no glow, no lens flare, no 3D render look, no glossy plastic shading, no photorealism, no stock-photo look, no infographic icons. No borders, frames, torn-paper edges, visible paper edge, white margins, square crop or centered composition.
