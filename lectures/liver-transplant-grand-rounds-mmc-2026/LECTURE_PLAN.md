# Liver Transplantation: When to Refer and What's New

Grand rounds at Maine Medical Center, 11/18/2026. One hour: about 45 minutes of talk, then questions. Built on the Nov 2025 Liver Transplant Basics deck ([LT-BASICS](../liver-transplant/2025-11-10%20Liver%20Transplant%20Basics%20Compressed.pptx)).

**Status:** first full build, 2026-10-27. 46 numbered slides in the talk copy with the Tufts slides (43 in this public copy), counting the cover as 1, two reveals, two appendix slides of sources. [Deck](Liver%20Transplantation%20When%20to%20Refer%20and%20Whats%20New.pptx), [PDF](LT-grand-rounds-MMC-2026-slides.pdf).

**Audience:** GI and hepatology faculty plus faculty, specialists, residents and fellows from across the hospital. Specialist room, so slide titles are clinical questions, paper slides show the title block and result together, and there are no "What do you think they found?" prompts. No patient case.

**Format:** specialist evidence review (like HYPO-NA and AKI in the source index of MY_STYLE.md).

## Parts

| Part | Slides | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Is it worth it? | 4-6 | SRTR 2024 survival figure, record US volume, Tufts volume (talk copy only) |
| When to refer | 7-14 | 2025 AASLD/AST referral triggers and MELD ≥12, where MELD 15 came from, LDLT benefit below 15, MELD 3.0 and women, frailty, what no longer excludes |
| From Maine to Boston | 15-19 | Where Tufts recipients live (talk copy only), acuity circles, the evaluation, what the referring team can do while the patient waits |
| Who can get a liver now | 20-31 | Early LT for alcohol-associated hepatitis (Mathurin, ACCELERATE-AH, QuickTrans, guidelines), US diagnosis mix, ACLF (CHANCE), exception points, TransMet and US colorectal data, iCCA |
| Where the livers come from | 32-38 | Donor types, DCD growth and machine perfusion, ischemic cholangiopathy, how the machines work, the three RCTs, living donors |
| Your patient had a transplant | 39-46 | Abnormal liver tests, MRCP vs ERCP, tacrolimus interactions, the primary care checklist from the 2025 non-graft guideline, take-home points |

Slide numbers above are for the talk copy. Cut first if long: the US diagnosis mix, iCCA.
