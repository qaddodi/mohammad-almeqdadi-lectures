# ALF and ACLF in the ICU: background image prompts

## Visual concept

The liver as the patient in an ICU at night. One anatomical liver, drawn the way a medical illustrator would, sitting in the low light of a unit after midnight. Nothing dramatic. It should feel like the start of a long call night, which is what the lecture is about.

## Palette

- Warm ivory paper (#F4EEE2) as the base
- Muted slate (#5E6B78) for shadow and ink wash
- Ochre (#B98A4A) and dusty rose (#B97D7D) for the liver itself
- Sage (#8FA08A) for the hepatic vessels and portal structures, lightly
- Cream (#EFE6D2) for highlights
- Localized dark indigo (#2E3550) only behind the focal subject, to suggest the unit at night

## Master style block

Clinical editorial watercolor over fine gray ink linework, on warm ivory textured cold-press paper. Loose, controlled washes with visible paper grain and soft blooms at the edges. Ink lines are thin, confident and anatomically correct, like a hand-drawn plate in an older surgical atlas. Muted slate, ochre, dusty rose, sage and cream, with a small localized area of dark indigo wash. One focal subject. Large calm negative space. Full-bleed composition with no border, frame, vignette edge or seam. 16:9, at least 1920×1080.

## Master negative prompt

No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, watermarks or signatures. No monitor readouts or digits. No faces, no patients, no hands, no blood, no gore, no surgical scene. No neon, no glow effects, no lens flare, no 3D render look, no glossy plastic shading, no photorealism, no stock-photo look, no infographic icons. No borders, frames, torn-paper edges or visible paper edge. Nothing placed in the right half of the image beyond paper texture and the faintest wash.

- **Purpose:** sets the subject (the liver) and the setting (the ICU at night) without any words, and leaves a clean area for a readable title.
- **Composition and quiet zone:** liver centered at about 25% from the left edge, roughly 40% of image width. The indigo wash stays within the left 45%. The right 55% is quiet: ivory paper grain, at most a very pale cream or slate wash, nothing with edges or detail. Even tonal value across the quiet zone so dark title text reads cleanly.
- **Continuity:** this is the master look for any later section backgrounds in this deck (same paper, same palette, same ink weight).
- **Clinical accuracy checks:**
  - Right lobe clearly larger than left lobe, falciform ligament dividing them on the anterior surface
  - Gallbladder fundus just visible below the inferior edge of the right lobe
  - Inferior vena cava and hepatic veins faint at the posterior-superior surface if shown at all
  - Smooth capsule, normal liver shape (this is not a cirrhotic, nodular liver, since ALF is usually a previously normal liver)
  - No organs other than the liver
- **Alt text:** "Watercolor illustration of a liver in dim night-time ICU light, on ivory paper."

### Full prompt

Clinical editorial watercolor illustration over fine gray ink linework, on warm ivory textured cold-press paper, 16:9 landscape, full-bleed with no border or frame. A single anatomically correct human liver, anterior view, placed in the left third of the image, about 40% of the image width. The right lobe is clearly larger than the left lobe, the falciform ligament runs down the anterior surface between them, and the fundus of the gallbladder just shows beneath the lower edge of the right lobe. The capsule is smooth and the shape is normal, not cirrhotic. The liver is painted in muted ochre and dusty rose washes with cream highlights, thin sage lines hinting at hepatic vessels, and confident thin gray ink contours in the style of an old surgical atlas plate. Behind the liver, a soft localized wash of dark indigo and muted slate suggests the dim light of an intensive care room at night; this wash blooms out from behind the organ and fades completely into ivory paper before the middle of the image. The right 55% of the image is calm negative space: plain ivory paper grain with at most a very pale cream wash, even in tone, no shapes, no lines, no detail, so a dark title can be set over it later. Visible paper texture throughout, soft watercolor blooms at wash edges, restrained and quiet mood. No text, letters, numbers, labels, watermarks or signatures. No monitors, readouts, faces, hands, patients, blood or surgical scene. No neon, glow, lens flare, 3D render look, glossy shading, photorealism or infographic icons. No borders, torn edges or visible paper edge.
