# Tachycardia-Induced Cardiomyopathy: background image prompts

## Visual concept

The heart and the history of the electrocardiogram. The cover shows an early string-galvanometer electrocardiograph from the 1910s, the kind of machine that recorded the first rhythm tracings in the years Gossage described a heart that failed from fast atrial fibrillation. The three section pages are the places where each patient was first seen: an ED bay (Patient A), a cardiology clinic room (Patient C) and a bedside table with a Holter monitor (Patient B). Five square spot illustrations sit on white slides beside tables.

## Palette

Same as the ALF and CHB decks: warm ivory paper #F4EEE2, muted slate #5E6B78, ochre #B98A4A, dusty rose #B97D7D, sage #8FA08A, cream #EFE6D2 highlights. Dark indigo #2E3550 only as a small local wash behind the cover subject. Wood and brass on the cover machine stay muted ochre, not shiny gold.

## Master style block

Clinical editorial watercolor over fine gray ink linework, on warm ivory textured cold-press paper. Loose, controlled washes with visible paper grain and soft blooms at the edges. Thin, confident, accurate ink lines, like a plate in an older medical atlas. Muted slate, ochre, dusty rose, sage and cream. One focal subject, large calm negative space. Full-bleed, no border, frame, vignette or seam. 16:9, at least 1920 x 1080.

## Master negative prompt

No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, watermarks or signatures. No readable ECG tracings, no waveforms with numbers, no monitor digits. No faces in detail, no blood, no gore. No cartoon hearts, heart symbols or glowing hearts. No neon, glow, lens flare, 3D render look, glossy shading, photorealism, stock-photo look or infographic icons. No borders, frames, torn edges or visible paper edge. No square crop, no white background on full-bleed images, no centered composition, no margins.

## Patient continuity sheet

Only Patient C appears as a person. The section pages show places, not people.

Patient C: 68-year-old man, average build, short gray hair, clean-shaven, thin wire glasses, light blue button-down shirt and khaki trousers. On his section page he is not shown, only the clinic room he sits in. On "6 months later" he is walking outdoors, relaxed, which is the point of the slide.

## Clinical accuracy checks

- **Cover electrocardiograph:** an early 1910s string-galvanometer ECG on a wooden bench: a long horizontal brass-and-iron galvanometer with a large electromagnet, a lamp housing at one end, a camera box with a slot for photographic paper at the other. Beside it, three porcelain or glass jars of saline used as limb electrodes. No patient, no modern parts, no screen.
- **ED bay:** an empty stretcher with a folded white sheet, a wall-mounted monitor with a dark screen (no readable waveform), an IV pole, a privacy curtain half drawn. No people, no blood.
- **Clinic room:** an exam table with paper, two plain chairs, a rolling 12-lead ECG cart with its cable bundle coiled on top. Window light. No people.
- **Holter monitor:** a small rectangular recorder about the size of a deck of cards, with five or seven thin colored lead wires ending in round adhesive electrode pads, lying on a bedside table next to a glass of water and a folded paper diary. No screen digits, no brand.
- **Echo machine:** a cart-based echocardiography machine with a dark screen, keyboard and a phased-array probe in its holder, beside a padded exam bed with a pillow. The screen is dark or shows only a soft gray fan shape with no detail.
- **EP lab:** a narrow procedure table under a large fluoroscopy C-arm, with a bank of dark monitors above. No people, no catheters drawn in detail.
- **Wearable defibrillator:** a garment-style vest with a flat electrode belt and a small monitor unit on a strap, folded over the back of a plain chair. Not an AED box, not paddles.
- **Patient C walking:** the man from the continuity sheet, walking on a park path, seen from the side, face only as a soft outline.
- **Smartwatch:** a plain smartwatch on a wrist resting on a table, dark screen. No ECG trace or numbers on the screen, no brand.
- **Spoilers:** none of the pictures shows an ECG, an echo frame or a number, so they cannot give away an answer. The ECGs and the echo image on the case slides come from Mohammad, not from Gemini.
