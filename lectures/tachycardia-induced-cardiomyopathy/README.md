# Tachycardia-Induced Cardiomyopathy

Draft. Cardiology talk for residents and fellows, built around three patients: flutter with 2:1 conduction in the ED, a 26% PVC burden, and rate-controlled persistent AF. One build.js makes both versions.

- `Tachycardia-Induced Cardiomyopathy.pptx` and `TIC-slides.pdf`: 60-minute talk, 55 numbered slides
- `Tachycardia-Induced Cardiomyopathy (30 min).pptx`: 30-minute cut, 41 numbered slides
- `LECTURE_PLAN.md`: outline, verified sources and open questions
- `tachycardia-induced-cardiomyopathy-background-image-prompts.md`: Gemini prompts for the cover, section pages and spot pictures
- `build/`: build.js and figures. `sh rebuild.sh` builds both decks. Slides inside `ext(() => {...})` appear only in the 60-minute version.

Paper screenshots come from PDFs Mohammad supplied (Gossage 1913: first page only). Dashed boxes are placeholders: de-identified ECGs and echo stills, and the Coleman 1971 title block.
