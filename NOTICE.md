## What credit means

If you reuse or adapt the guides, a deck, or parts of one, do three things:

1. Name Mohammad Almeqdadi as the author.
2. Link to this repository: https://github.com/qaddodi/mohammad-almeqdadi-lectures
3. Say it is CC BY 4.0, and say if you changed it.

An attribution line you can paste into a slide, a handout, a document or a repo:

> Lecture style and materials by Mohammad Almeqdadi, https://github.com/qaddodi/mohammad-almeqdadi-lectures, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Adapted by [your name].

For a deck, a line on the title slide or the last slide is enough. For a guide or a repo, put it in the README. If you build a new lecture by following MY_STYLE.md and AUTHOR_GUIDE.md and copy none of the wording or slides, the credit is a courtesy, not a requirement. He would still like the mention.

- **Figures, tables, screenshots and excerpts from papers, guidelines, textbooks, news pages and other people's slides.** Many decks show these with a source line. They belong to their authors and publishers. This license does not apply to them, and you need your own permission or a fair-use basis to reuse them. The same goes for logos and institution branding.
- **Images made with an AI image tool.** Some covers, section pages and spot pictures were generated. They are included with the decks, but he claims only whatever rights the law gives him in them.
- **His name and affiliations.** The license lets you reuse the material with credit. It does not say he endorses your talk or your changes.
- **Clinical content.** The decks are teaching material from a specific talk on a specific date. Check anything you plan to use clinically against current guidelines.
